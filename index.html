About Talk Your Mind
Empowering young creatives since 2020 in Ngqeleni, Eastern Cape, South Africa.

Talk Your Mind is a youth empowerment platform established in 2020 in Ngqeleni, Eastern Cape, South Africa. Our mission is to create a supportive environment for young creatives to express themselves, showcase their talents, and thrive. We emphasize creativity as a means of self-expression and personal development, focusing on uplifting the next generation of artists, entrepreneurs, and innovators.

TYMTRENDZKORNER, our flagship podcast, highlights the stories and achievements of young black creatives, entrepreneurs, and change-makers. We celebrate black culture and amplify the voices of black female leaders in male-dominated fields. The podcast covers trending news, fresh voices, and cultural shifts while fostering a supportive community that inspires and creates opportunities.

We are more than just a platform; we're a movement dedicated to empowering young creatives in Ngqeleni and beyond. Our community-driven initiative provides a supportive ecosystem that fosters growth, creativity, and innovation through tailored promotions, marketing, event hosting, and entertainment services. By partnering with us, you join a community that amplifies your voice, celebrates your achievements, and elevates your brand.
